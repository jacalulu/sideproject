Mermaid Dots — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Mermaid Dots is a puzzle game for kids and families. It is built to be played, not to collect information.

What we collect

Nothing. Mermaid Dots does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or usage data. There are no accounts, no sign-ins, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party services of any kind.

What stays on your device

Your game progress, chosen character, and sound preference are saved locally on your device so the game can remember where you left off. This information never leaves your device and is deleted if you delete the app.

Network access

The app works entirely offline. It makes no network requests.

Children's privacy

Mermaid Dots is suitable for children. Because the app collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from children.

Purchases and ads

There are none. No in-app purchases, no subscriptions, no advertisements.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new "last updated" date.
